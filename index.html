Good to Know
  • Leave no trace — pack out everything we bring in, douse the fire fully before bed.
  • Check the campground's fire & firework rules before the 4th; burn bans vary by site.
  • Plan a gallon of water per person per day — it goes fast in July heat.
  • Assign a cooler captain so the ice doesn't melt by Saturday.
  • Charge everything Friday morning — power's a luxury out there.
